Data Deletion Instructions

You can stop our access to your Facebook Page or Instagram account and have your stored data deleted at any time.

Step 1: Remove the app from Facebook

In Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), find the Ardent app, and choose Remove. This ends our access to your accounts immediately.

Step 2: Ask us to delete your data

Email support@afterlifeinteractive.com from the address you used with us and ask us to delete your data. We will delete the stored connection details and access tokens for your accounts, and confirm by email within 30 days.

Posts already published on your Page or Instagram account belong to you and stay there unless you delete them yourself.

See also our Privacy Policy.
