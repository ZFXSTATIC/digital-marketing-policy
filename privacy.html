Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Ardent, a division of Afterlife Interactive LLC ("we", "us") runs a private tool that publishes videos and captions to the Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts of the organizations we do marketing work for. This policy explains what information the tool handles and why.

Information we handle

  • Account connection data. When an account owner signs in with Facebook and grants access, we receive the names and IDs of the Facebook Pages and linked Instagram professional accounts they choose, and an access token that lets us post to them.
  • Content we publish. The videos and captions we upload on behalf of an account owner, and the status of each post (for example, posted or failed).

We do not collect passwords. We do not read private messages, and we do not collect personal data about the followers or visitors of any connected Page or account.

How we use it

We use this information only to publish content that the account owner has asked us to publish, and to show us whether each post succeeded. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and security

Access tokens are encrypted when stored. Access to the tool is protected by a login and is limited to us. Uploaded videos are kept only as long as needed to publish and review the post.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent information. Content and tokens are sent only to Meta (Facebook and Instagram) as needed to publish, and are not shared with anyone else except where required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep connection data for as long as the account owner wants us to manage their account. Owners can end that at any time, and we will delete their stored data. See the data deletion instructions.

Children

This tool is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.

Who we are

Ardent is operated by Afterlife Interactive LLC.
